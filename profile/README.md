SNGLL Labs LLC is a blockchain innovation company developing SNGLL™, a digital ecosystem that transforms how customers interact, earn, and engage within the Sub N Grill experience. Our mission is to integrate blockchain technology directly into real-world environments — starting with the restaurant and hospitality sector — to create smarter, more rewarding connections between brands and their communities.

Founded as part of Vanguard Global Holdings LLC, and operating under license from American Sub Grill LLC, SNGLL Labs was built to expand the Sub N Grill brand into the digital economy through the SNGLL™ token — the central element of our ecosystem.

Within Sub N Grill locations, SNGLL™ serves as a utility and rewards token, allowing customers to earn, redeem, and stake tokens for exclusive benefits such as discounts, access to private events, digital collectibles, and other real-world perks. This system creates a seamless bridge between dining experiences and decentralized participation, giving every customer the opportunity to become part of the brand’s growing digital community.

Through SNGLL™, we are connecting real-life experiences with digital value, turning every meal, purchase, and interaction into part of a broader ecosystem that rewards loyalty, strengthens engagement, and builds lasting relationships between the brand and its guests.
